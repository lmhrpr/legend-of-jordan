#   S        stairs: put one anywhere in a plateau's cliff, they're cut up that column

#   a-m      a cave entrance cut down into the ground (like the steps on top of a plateau)
#   < > ^ v  open edge (no parapet): Jordan can jump off it that way. Put it on the edge cell.
